Build the scenario definition table for our NPI modelling paper. We model four
interventions in an age-structured contact model: school closure, 50% reduction in
workplace contacts, closure of hospitality venues (which we treat as 60% of "other"
contacts), and shielding of over-65s (reducing their non-household contacts by 75%).
We also model all four together. Interventions start on 1 March and last 8 weeks.
