Draft the introduction for our paper. We estimated the incubation period of mpox clade Ib
from 212 travel-associated cases with known exposure windows, reported to national public
health agencies in 2024–2025, using a doubly interval-censored Bayesian model. Earlier
incubation period estimates come from the 2022 clade IIb outbreak, which spread mainly
through sexual networks in Europe and the Americas. Our estimate: mean 8.1 days (95% CrI
7.2–9.1), 95th percentile 17.3 days (15.1–20.4). The current 21-day contact monitoring
period would capture 99.1% (98.2–99.6) of incubation periods under our estimate.
