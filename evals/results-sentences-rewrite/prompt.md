Tighten these three results sentences from our influenza forecasting paper (target: PNAS):

1. "The ensemble model performed well across all seasons."
2. "Forecast accuracy decreased substantially with horizon."
3. "The model predicted the peak week to within about 2 weeks in most seasons."

Our numbers: ensemble mean log score −2.41 vs −2.87 for the best individual model and
−3.95 for the seasonal-baseline model, across 7 seasons; mean absolute error 0.31, 0.58,
0.92 and 1.24 percentage points at 1–4 week horizons; peak week within 1 week in 5 of 7
seasons and within 3 weeks in all 7.
